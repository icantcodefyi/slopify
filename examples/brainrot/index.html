Johannes Gutenberg (W)

Around 1440 in Mainz, Germany, Johannes Gutenberg invented the printing press with movable metal type. He based the press on a wine press (real). He printed the Bible. It never once asked to update its drivers.

Printer Lore 📜

  • 1938 Chester Carlson invents xerography (photocopying) in Queens, NY. Mewing level: early.
