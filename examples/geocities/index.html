ABOUT THE HOTDOG (a summary. sorry it is long)

  • The hotdog is a frank inside a bun. The frank goes IN the bun. I have seen people do it the other way. I will not name them (Tyler).
  • Franks come in packs of 10. Buns come in packs of 8. I wrote to my congressman about this in 1987. He has not written back. Sorry to bring it up again.
  • A hotdog is NOT a sandwich. A sandwich has two breads. A hotdog has one bread, folded, like a taco. Is a hotdog a taco? No. Sorry. It is a hotdog.
  • Ketchup: allowed. Mustard: encouraged. Relish: the green one. Chicago people put a whole salad and a pickle spear on it, and that is their right as Americans.
