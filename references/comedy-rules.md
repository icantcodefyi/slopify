# Comedy rules

Visual slop is free. Writing slop takes effort. These are the formulas that make
the best parody sites funny. Use them deliberately.

## 1. The self-undercutting claim (use everywhere)
A confident claim, then a qualifier that kills it.
- "unlimited* thumbnails" … "*limit 10"
- "here is 0.5% off. final offer. (not final)"
- "Results <u>not guaranteed</u> but definately probably good."
- "Prize Wheel (not rigged)" (every slice is the same prize)
- "Sign Up Free!!" → the plan is $0.99/forever

## 2. Fake precision
Tiny, oddly specific numbers sound like real data.
- "trained on 14 thumbnails and one (1) MrBeast video"
- "now with 3% MORE AI!!!"
- "Thumbnail generated in only 47 minutes!!"
- "AI confidence: 12%"
- "red arrows increase views by 4000%*. *source: me"

## 3. Deflating feature descriptions
Real feature name, then a literal description of what it actually does, badly.
- **Prompt to Thumbnail**: "Type words. Get picture. Sometimes the picture is about the words."
- **Arrow Engine™**: "Industry leading red arrow placement. Arrows point at nothing in particular."
- **Title Generator**: "Every title is 'YOU WON'T BELIEVE THIS (GONE WRONG)'. It works tho."

## 4. Pathetic social proof
Real-sounding numbers that are actually tiny or obviously fake.
- "#1 thumbnail AI in my apartment"
- "I went from 3 subscribers to 4 subscribers. Life changing. 10/10"
- Gallery captions with view counts: "4 views", "0 views", "1.2M views (bot)", "removed by YouTube"
- "Trusted by: MrBreast, PewDiePie's cousin, Markiplier (fan acct), my mom, Steve"

## 5. Steve (the recurring nobody)
One human who does the enterprise tier by hand. Bring him back at least 3 times.
- Pricing: "Enterprise: Call Steve. Steve makes them by hand. in MS Paint. Steve is not available weekends"
- Trusted-by logo row, fake phone call popup ("SlopCorp Phone 2003"), virus list (`steve.exe`)

## 6. The narrator's desperation (meta layer)
The page knows a broke freelancer made it, and keeps asking for work.
- Banner: "💸 I redesigned X for FREE and still no job offer. Please buy X with my affiliate link"
- Welcome popup buttons: "OK" / "OK (i will hire him)"
- A fake ad for the narrator's other project ("The interactive sand experience is now live!!!")
- "Get DIRECT CONNECTION feedback from notnarrator™ (who is not the narrator). $0.99 per 7 seconds, rounded up to the nearest hour"

## 7. Dark patterns, played as jokes
Copy real growth-hacking UI, but make the manipulation completely obvious.
- Cookie: "We use cookies, crumbs, and ur webcam…". Buttons: Accept / **ACCEPT HARDER** / reject (tiny, gray)
- Exit intent: "ur leaving?? 😭😭😭"
- Chatbot: "SlopBot (online-ish)", input placeholder "type here (i wont read it)"
- Clippy: "It looks like ur trying to close this. That feature is coming soon!"
- Error dialogs: "Error: Success." "Task failed successfully." "Keyboard not found. Press F1 to continue."

## Spelling & voice
Misspell **only well-known words**, so the reader instantly spots it as a joke:
Exampels, Reveiws, POPULER, definately, recieve, seperate, garantee, sucess, buisness,
teh, loose (for lose), alot. Plus internet-speak: u, ur, pls, tho, lol, (lie), (bot), (emotional).
Rules: misspell ~1 word per section, NOT every word. Capitalize hype words: VIRAL, FREE, NOW!!!
Use 3+ exclamation marks on CTAs, and emoji in pairs (🔥🔥, 💰💰💰).

## Brainrot seasoning (sprinkle, don't drown)
Ohio, skibidi, rizz, gyatt, sigma, "fanum tax" (on a discount), "aura +1000", "mewing", NPC,
"it's giving", "no cap", "only in Ohio". At most one or two per section. Brainrot lines date
quickly; the formulas above don't.

## Social proof ticker templates
`🔔 Someone in {place} just {action} {n} seconds ago`
Places: Ohio, a Walmart parking lot, the Moon, ur house, Steve's basement, Florida (again)
Actions: bought Pro, cried, clicked the red arrow, became famous (lie), asked for a refund (denied)
