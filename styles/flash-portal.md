# Flash game portal (Newgrounds / Miniclip / Addicting Games, 2005)

**Vibe:** a free online games site. The product is a "game", there's an embedded "game" that's barely playable, plus ads everywhere.

## Layout skeleton
Dark or orange header with a chunky logo ("SLOPCLIP — free online games!!"), category tabs (Action | Puzzle | Dress-up |
Hotdog | Steve), a "Top Games" sidebar with thumbnails and star ratings, and a central **game frame** (640×480 with
a black border) containing a tiny playable JS game about the mascot (click the hotdog, dodge the cone, catch falling
items). Below: "Rate this game ★★★★☆", "Add to favorites", "Comments (4,812)", a high-score table (Steve holds every slot).
Banner ads (728×90 and 160×600 skyscraper) for absurd things ("PUNCH THE MONKEY WIN $20", "Your PC is SLOW").

## Type & palette
- Chunky headings `"Arial Black","Impact"` with a heavy stroke; body Verdana 11px
- Newgrounds-dark: `#111` + orange `#fda238`; or Miniclip-white: white + navy + yellow badges
- Game thumbnails: 100×75 boxes with emoji + bold label, a "NEW" corner ribbon or "HOT 🔥"

## Signature components
- Pre-roll "ad" before the game starts: a 5-second countdown that's actually 15 seconds ("skip in 5…6…7")
- "Loading game… 99%" with a spinning gear
- Game over screen: "You scored 3. Steve scored 4,000,000. Steve is always online."
- "Get the Shockwave plugin" modal
- Fake "Punch the monkey" banner that responds to clicks

## Voice
Pre-teen hype + adware. "free online games!!! no download!!! (download required)".

## Best gags
the playable mini-game (core), creep (high score), errors (plugin crash), virus (from banner ad), proof ("xX_Steve_Xx beat your score"), rain on win
