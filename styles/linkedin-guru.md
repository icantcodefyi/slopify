# LinkedIn guru / Corporate Memphis

**Vibe:** a thought-leader's personal brand site + course. Clean on the surface, completely unhinged in the copy.

## Layout skeleton
Feels like a modern SaaS page that's slightly off. Light gray `#f3f2ef` feed background, LinkedIn-ish top bar
(logo "SlopedIn", search "Search for synergy", nav icons with notification badge 99+). Profile card hero
(cover banner, round headshot with #OPENTOWORK ring, headline "CEO | Founder | Thought Leader | {Mascot} Evangelist |
Ex-Steve | Dad"). Then a **feed of posts** (one-sentence-per-line broetry, "Agree?", "I'm humbled to announce"),
each with reactions 👍❤️👏💡 and "see more" that expands to something unnecessary. Sidebar: "People also viewed"
(all the same person), "Promoted" course ad. Ends with a $997 course pricing page with Corporate Memphis art.

## Type & palette
- `-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`; LinkedIn blue `#0a66c2`; green `#057642` for "open to work"
- Corporate Memphis illustrations as inline SVG: flat blobs, purple/orange skin tones, absurdly long arms, tiny heads
- Rounded 8px cards, soft shadows. This era is "clean slop". The cursedness lives in the copy and the illustrations.

## Signature components
- Broetry post: "I fired my best employee today.\n\nHe was a {mascot}.\n\nHere's what it taught me about B2B sales 🧵"
- Endorsement counter: "Skills: Breathing (99+ endorsements)"
- "X viewed your profile" notifications (proof) all from recruiters at "Steve Inc."
- Engagement-bait poll that sums to 140%
- "Repost ♻️ if you agree". The button reposts the post again within the feed.
- Course funnel: "From 0 to Thought Leader in 47 days"

## Voice
Humblebrag, fake vulnerability, every story ends in a B2B lesson. "Let that sink in."

## Best gags
proof (profile views), creep (followers), toast ("Steve endorsed you for Excel"), exit, cookie (as "We value your data. A lot.")
