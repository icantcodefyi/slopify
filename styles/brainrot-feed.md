# Gen-alpha brainrot feed

**Vibe:** a website that thinks it's TikTok. Short attention span, split screen, word-by-word captions, Italian brainrot.

## Layout skeleton
Mobile-first, even on desktop: a centered 9:16 phone frame (max 420px) with **vertical scroll-snap
sections** (`scroll-snap-type: y mandatory`), one "video" per screen. Each slide: top half is the content,
bottom half is fake **Subway Surfers / Minecraft parkour** gameplay (a CSS-animated runner on train tracks,
or a scrolling block grid) "to keep you focused". Right rail of action icons (❤️ 4.2M, 💬, ↗, 🎵 spinning disc).
Big word-by-word caption in the middle (white, black stroke, the active word yellow). Bottom: @handle + "original sound - {mascot}".
On desktop: the sides show a frantic "For You" sidebar and an "Aura" meter.

## Type & palette
- Captions: `"Montserrat","Arial Black",sans-serif` 900 weight, `-webkit-text-stroke: 2px #000`, uppercase
- Black background, TikTok cyan `#25F4EE` / red `#FE2C55` offset glitch text shadow
- Emoji everywhere: 💀😭🔥🗣️🥀

## Signature components
- **Aura meter**: +/- aura points for every click ("-500 aura: clicked the cookie banner")
- **Italian brainrot cast** as the "team": Tralalero Tralala, Bombardiro Crocodilo, Tung Tung Tung Sahur, Ballerina Cappuccina, plus the mascot as a new character ("Hotdoggino Spaghettino")
- Vocabulary: skibidi, rizz, gyatt, sigma, mewing, fanum tax, Ohio, "6-7", "lowkey", "it's giving", "chat is this real"
- Fake live chat scrolling up ("chat is this real", "W", "L", "ratio")
- A "brainrot level" quiz that always results in "Terminal (Ohio)"

## Voice
"chat. CHAT. this {mascot} is NOT real 💀💀" Short, loud, captioned, cut every 2 seconds.
