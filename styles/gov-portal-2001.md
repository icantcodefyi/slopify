# Government / university portal 2001

**Vibe:** an official, joyless, table-based site nobody has updated since 2001. The comedy is the deadpan bureaucracy.

## Layout skeleton
Fixed 760px `<table>` layout. Header: crest/seal (CSS circle with tiny text "DEPT. OF {MASCOT} AFFAIRS · EST. 1997"),
department name in Times, gray utility links (Text Only | Site Map | Accessibility | Español (coming 2003)).
Left column: a long bulleted nav of nested links in blue underlined Arial ("Forms & Publications", "Form 27-B/6").
Center: "Latest News" with dated items from 2001, "Important Notice" in a red-bordered box, a giant
service flow: Step 1 of 14. Right column: "Quick Links", a GIF "Y2K Ready" badge, "Last updated: 03/14/2001".
Footer: legal paragraph, "This site is best viewed in Netscape Navigator 4.7 at 800x600", webmaster email.

## Type & palette
- Headings `"Times New Roman",serif` navy `#003366`; body `Arial` 12px `#000`; links `#0000EE` / visited `#551A8B`
- Gray `#eeeeee` sidebars, `1px solid #999` table borders, `<hr>` with shading, a teal or maroon header band
- No rounded corners. No animation except one "NEW!" gif (CSS blink)

## Signature components
- The **form from hell**: fields like "Mother's maiden name (of your {mascot})", "Reason for existing", a
  required checkbox "I am not a hotdog", Submit says "Please print, sign, and fax to Dwayne"
- Queue: "You are #48,201 in line. Estimated wait: 3 business decades" (progress bar going backwards)
- PDF links labeled "(PDF, 488 MB)", "Form 1A (requires Form 1B, which requires Form 1A)"
- Office hours: "Tues 10:00–10:05, closed for lunch"
- "This page has been accessed 000,004 times since 1998"

## Voice
Deadpan official passive voice. "Citizens are reminded that…" "Failure to comply may result in a stern look."

## Best gags
creep (queue number), errors ("Session timed out" after 3 s), captcha (bureaucratic), cookie (as "Privacy Notice per Act 1997-B"), toast as "NOTICE"
