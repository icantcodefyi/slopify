# MySpace 2006

**Vibe:** someone "customized" their profile with copied CSS. Emo, glittery, very personal.

## Layout skeleton
Fake site header (a "MySpace"-ish logo for something like "SlopSpace", search bar, nav links: Home | Browse |
Search | Invite | Film | Mail | Blog | Favorites | Forum | Groups | Events | Videos | Music). Then
**two columns**: left = profile pic, "Online Now!" gif, mood, contact box (Add to Friends / Send Message /
Block User), interests table, "Details" table; right = "{name} is in your extended network", blurbs
("About me:", "Who I'd like to meet:"), **Top 8 friends grid**, comments wall with timestamps.

## Type & palette
- Verdana/Arial 10–12px everywhere except the custom header (glitter/Impact/"Lucida Handwriting")
- Black or hot-pink tiled background (skulls, stars, leopard print as a repeating CSS pattern), the
  table boxes stay in that `#6698CB` blue/`#FF9933` orange MySpace default under the user's chaos
- Glitter text: animated background-clip text with a sparkle gradient

## Signature components
- Music player box that "autoplays" (after first click) a song with an absurd title; can't be paused
- Top 8 drama: "Tom" is #1; one friend is "Steve (we're not talking)"
- Comments: "thx 4 the add!!", "PC4PC?", "omg ur pic is so cute", with fake 2006 timestamps
- Mood: ":( drained", currently listening: "{mascot} - My Chemical Romance (nightcore)"
- Blinkies and "rawr means i love u in dinosaur", "~*~xXx_name_xXx~*~" headings
- Survey "bulletins" ("50 THINGS ABOUT ME!!! repost if u dare")

## Voice
lowercase, sparkle tildes ~*~, "xoxo", "rawr", dramatic but low stakes.

## Best gags
creep (profile views counter), proof ("X just added you as a friend"), chat (AIM-style), roll, rain (sparkles), konami
